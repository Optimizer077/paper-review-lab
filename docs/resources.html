HOW TO USE THE SUPPORT

Read with purpose.
Keep your judgment.

Start with a question about your draft. Read the relevant guidance, apply a bounded revision, and check that the science, uncertainty and terminology still agree across sections.

These are selected resources from original providers, not a universal ranking. Page checks do not establish that a course was completed, a book was read, or a tool was benchmarked.

The 10,000-paper library retains its 2022 cutoff. Support guides and current reporting instructions are maintained separately and may be newer. Check the relevant version and study design before treating guidance as a requirement.
