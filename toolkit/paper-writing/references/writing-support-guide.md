# Use writing support for the next concrete revision

The [annotated catalog](writing-resources.md) contains 42 selected primary-provider resources. The [searchable resource hub](https://optimizer077.github.io/paper-review-lab/resources.html) filters them by task, category, resource type and contribution profile.

Choose the issue in the supplied draft before opening a guide. Identify one relevant action, apply it to an inspected passage, and reconcile the result with the factual and terminology record. Avoid rewriting a whole manuscript to imitate a sample introduction or a generic prose score.

## Route the task

| Current need | Useful starting point | Apply it this way |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Make an abstract answer the actual question | [Abstract](https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/eecs/commkit/abstract/) | Identify the problem, approach, finding and significance. Check each statement against the supplied study; do not add an outcome just to complete a suggested structure. |
| Explain why the study is needed | [Journal Article: Introduction](https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/be/commkit/journal-article-introduction/) | Connect established knowledge, the specific unresolved issue and the research objective. Preserve the distinction between importance, novelty and measured improvement. |
| Plan the argument across sections | [Ten simple rules for structuring papers](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005619) | Build an outline around the contribution and its support. Use paragraph and section advice as a way to inspect logic, adapting it to theory, resources, negative findings or empirical work. |
| Diagnose reader difficulty | [Duke Graduate School Scientific Writing Resource](https://sites.duke.edu/scientificwriting/) | Check whether the intended reader can recover what each sentence and paragraph means before changing vocabulary or shortening sentences. |
| Improve phrasing without changing the science | [Academic Phrasebank](https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk/) | Select a language function such as comparison, caution or definition. Adapt generic phrasing to the actual claim, keeping units, negation and uncertainty intact. |
| Synthesize literature around a question | [Ten Simple Rules for Writing a Literature Review](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003149) | Organize sources around the review question and disagreements. A writing guide does not turn a convenience reading list into a systematic review. |
| Choose reporting guidance for the design | [EQUATOR reporting guideline library](https://equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/) | Find the checklist for the actual study design and article type. Use it to locate missing reporting details; it does not validate the study or create evidence. |
| Report clinical trials, observational studies or reviews | [PRISMA 2020 statement and checklists](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020-statement) | Use PRISMA for the relevant systematic-review report. For trials choose CONSORT, for observational research STROBE, for prediction models TRIPOD+AI and for animal studies ARRIVE. Inspect the applicable version and extensions. |
| Make a display carry a specific comparison | [Ten Simple Rules for Better Figures (2014)](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003833) | State what the reader should learn from the figure, choose an appropriate representation, and check labels, units, uncertainty and caption scope against the data. |
| Prepare display files for a target journal | [Nature research figure guide: preparing figures](https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/preparing-figures-our-specifications/) | Use the publisher specifications only when that venue is relevant. File dimensions, formats and accessibility checks are distinct from whether a figure supports the scientific claim. |
| Keep citations connected to inspected sources | [The Basics: Zotero Quick Start Guide](https://www.zotero.org/support/quick_start_guide) | Maintain citation records and attachments, check imported metadata, and inspect the source passage for consequential claims. A library entry or formatted reference is not evidence of entailment. |
| Record contributions and truthful disclosures | [CRediT contributor roles and implementation guidance](https://credit.niso.org/implementing-credit/) | Use the taxonomy to discuss actual contributor roles. Resolve authorship eligibility with the applicable journal and institutional policy; a role label is not automatic authorship. |
| Answer a reviewer with verifiable revisions | [Ten simple rules for writing a response to reviewers](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005730) | Connect the concern to an answer, the actual revision and its location. Distinguish completed changes from proposed work, and support justified disagreement. |
| Control terminology mechanically | [Vale: Introduction](https://docs.vale.sh/) | Configure agreed style or terminology checks. Review every alert in context; a prose linter cannot establish conceptual identity, evidence support or scientific correctness. |
| Build or count the manuscript | [TeXcount: Introduction](https://app.uio.no/ifi/texcount/intro.html) | Use documented counting rules for the intended LaTeX source. Check the target venue counting convention separately; successful counting or compilation does not certify the paper. |

## Manuals and courses

For sustained study, the catalog includes the Craft of Research, Writing Science, Writing Science in Plain English, Writing for Computer Science, and How to Write and Publish a Scientific Paper. These entries were checked through publisher listings or the author’s book information; the catalog does not imply a full reading. Use a library, authorized copy or available preview for the relevant chapter. Do not cite a book as inspected evidence on the basis of its title or description.

Stanford’s Writing in the Sciences and the Nature Masterclasses research-paper course provide structured learning options. The checked pages establish their published descriptions and access conditions; no course completion or independent effectiveness claim is made.

## Fit the support to the contribution

- **Empirical ML:** inspect methods, comparisons, uncertainty, data/code availability and the actual venue requirements. A checklist is a reporting aid rather than proof that a design is valid.
- **Theory:** use argument, notation, assumptions, proof structure and reader-understanding guidance. Do not penalize a theorem paper for lacking experiments it does not claim.
- **Clinical research:** select the actual design and reporting extension before using a checklist. Keep populations, endpoints, inference and disclosures tied to study records.
- **Systematic reviews:** use the review-specific search, selection, appraisal and synthesis requirements. The historical paper library is a convenience resource index and cannot serve as a completed systematic search.

## Use tools within their documented role

Overleaf, Pandoc and Quarto support document preparation; Zotero and JabRef support reference workflows; TeXcount supports counting; LanguageTool, Writefull and Vale support aspects of language or style. Choose tools that fit the author’s existing environment. Inspect the linked documentation for current features, access and data handling before moving manuscript content into a service. No accounts, subscriptions or uploads are needed to browse this catalog.

A grammar suggestion can change scientific meaning. Compare edited passages with the original claims, numbers, comparators and confidence. A bibliography tool can preserve a wrong citation record. Verify consequential source metadata and the passage being cited. Keep a mechanical warning separate from a supported scientific defect.

## Interpret the verification honestly

The JSON catalog records the exact resource and verification URLs, date, inspected scope, use, access and limitations. Most entries were checked through primary page content. Book entries disclose publisher or author metadata. COPE and ACM entries disclose indexed primary excerpts where direct retrieval was restricted. This verification does not constitute source authentication, a tool benchmark, a completed course or a claim that a book was read.

These resources were selected for original-provider provenance, direct manuscript relevance and actionable scope, not an objective global ranking. They include current instructions that may postdate 2022. The historical example-paper cutoff remains unchanged at 2022, with 10,000 citation records and its separately disclosed inspection history.

Use [venue profiles](venue-profiles.md) for exact submission requirements, [the evaluation protocol](evaluation-protocol.md) for independent observation and disagreement, and [aggressive review](aggressive-review.md) only when a scored assessment is requested. These resources do not add automatic penalties or substitute for criterion evidence.
